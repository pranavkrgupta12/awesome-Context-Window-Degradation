# Datasets and Benchmarks

This topic is primarily evaluated through **benchmarks and test suites** rather than one universally adopted dataset. The following are relevant categories/resources to investigate.

## Needle-in-a-Haystack (NIAH)

A family of long-context retrieval evaluations in which a target piece of information is placed inside a large amount of distracting context.

**Use:** Testing whether an LLM can retrieve information at different context lengths and positions.

## NoLiMa

A long-context evaluation approach designed to move beyond simple literal matching and test retrieval that requires latent associations.

**Use:** Evaluating whether long-context models genuinely retrieve information rather than relying on lexical overlap.

## DENIAHL

An evaluation/benchmark resource examining how in-context features influence Needle-in-a-Haystack performance.

**Use:** Studying the effect of feature placement and density on long-context retrieval.
