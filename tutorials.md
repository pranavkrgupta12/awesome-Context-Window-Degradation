# Tutorials and Learning Resources

## 1. Hugging Face NLP Course

Practical learning material for transformers, NLP, datasets, and modern language-model workflows.

https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course/

## 2. Stanford CS224N

Graduate-level Natural Language Processing course covering neural NLP and transformer-based methods.

https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/

## 3. Stanford CS25

Lectures and talks focused on transformers and modern language-model research.

https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs25/

## 4. Retrieval-Augmented Generation Survey

A useful scholarly introduction to RAG concepts and architectures.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10997

## 5. The Transformer Architecture

The original Transformer paper is useful background for understanding self-attention and long-context processing.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
